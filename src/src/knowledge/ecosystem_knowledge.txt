AI FOR ECOSYSTEM SUSTAINABILITY

Vegetation and Ecosystem Monitoring
Vegetation monitoring helps understand changes in plant cover, ecosystem conditions,
forest resources and biodiversity. Environmental indicators can be analyzed using
machine learning to support ecosystem monitoring.

Forest Conservation
Forest ecosystems provide habitat for biodiversity and contribute to ecosystem
stability. Conservation activities can include forest monitoring, restoration,
afforestation and protection of natural habitats.

Biodiversity Protection
Biodiversity protection focuses on maintaining species, habitats and ecosystem
variety. Environmental data can support identification of patterns that may require
further ecological investigation.

Climate Change
Climate-related indicators such as temperature, rainfall, carbon dioxide emissions,
sea-level rise and extreme weather events can influence ecosystem conditions.
Monitoring these indicators can support environmental assessment.

Sustainable Ecosystem Management
Sustainable ecosystem management combines environmental information with responsible
decision-making. Machine learning predictions should be treated as decision-support
information and validated using reliable ecological data and expert knowledge.

SDG 13 - Climate Action
Climate action includes efforts to understand and respond to climate-related risks,
reduce environmental impacts and improve resilience.

SDG 15 - Life on Land
Life on Land focuses on protecting terrestrial ecosystems, sustainably managing
forests, combating land degradation and biodiversity loss, and supporting ecosystem
restoration.

Responsible AI for Environmental Applications
AI predictions should not be treated as final ecological decisions. Predictions
should be interpreted together with data quality, environmental context and expert
knowledge.
